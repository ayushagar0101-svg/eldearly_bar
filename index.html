About the Product

Why the Elderly Nutrition Bar Was Formulated

A Changing Body, A Changing Diet

Ageing is a natural and continuous process associated with gradual changes in physical, physiological and nutritional requirements. Changes in appetite, digestion, metabolism, physical activity, chewing ability and swallowing may affect food intake among older adults.

A Soft, Nutrient-Dense Concept

The Elderly Nutrition Bar is proposed as a convenient, nutrient-dense and soft-textured supplementary snack designed to provide a combination of energy, protein, healthy fats, carbohydrates, dietary fibre, vitamins and minerals.

Built From Whole Ingredients

The formulation combines nuts, seeds, ragi, protein oats, dates, raisins, moringa, amla powder and cinnamon — chosen for both nutritional contribution and natural binding properties.

Process

Preparation Process

1

Selection and Weighing

Ingredients are selected and weighed according to the formulation.

2

Cleaning and Sorting

Ingredients are checked and cleaned to remove unwanted materials.

3

Roasting

Nuts and seeds are roasted appropriately to improve flavour and aroma.

4

Cooling and Grinding

The roasted ingredients are cooled and ground to achieve a suitable texture.

5

Binding Mixture

Dates and raisins are processed into a fruit-based mixture that acts as a natural binder.

6

Mixing

Ground ingredients are combined with ragi, protein oats, moringa, amla and cinnamon.

7

Forming

The mixture is pressed and shaped into bars.

8

Setting and Packaging

The bars are allowed to set, cooled and individually packaged.

Texture & Product Design

Designed for Easy Chewing

Some elderly individuals may experience dental or chewing difficulties. The proposed bar is therefore designed with these qualities in mind:

  • Soft texture
  • Cohesive structure
  • Easy-to-chew consistency
  • Pleasant mouthfeel
